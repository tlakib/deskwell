# DeskWell Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

DeskWell is a free, open-source desk wellness reminder app for Windows, macOS and Google Chrome, made by Md Ashik Ali.

## What DeskWell collects

Nothing. DeskWell has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

- Your settings, reminder schedule and history are stored only on your own device.
- DeskWell never records keystrokes, screens, the clipboard, your camera, your microphone or your browsing.
- DeskWell does not send any information to any server. It only opens a web page when you click a link yourself, such as the LinkedIn link in About.

## Sharing

Because DeskWell collects no personal data, it shares none with anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling DeskWell removes the app and the data it keeps on your device.

## Children

DeskWell does not collect information from anyone, including children.
